Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_rolling_corr

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Computes 126-day and 252-day rolling correlations for pairs of global equity indices (S&P 500, DAX, FTSE 100). Visualizes the time-varying nature of correlations, highlighting regime shifts during the GFC (2008), European debt crisis (2011), and COVID-19 (2020). Demonstrates that constant-correlation assumptions are empirically rejected in most financial data.

Keywords: rolling correlation, time-varying correlation, equity markets, financial crisis, contagion, correlation dynamics

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: yfinance API (^GSPC, ^GDAXI, ^FTSE), 2004-2024

Output: ch5b_rolling_correlations.pdf

Example: S&P 500-DAX rolling correlation ranges from 0.30 in calm periods to above 0.85 during GFC, motivating dynamic conditional correlation models.
