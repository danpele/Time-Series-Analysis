Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_crisis_boxplot

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Creates boxplots comparing the distribution of pairwise correlations across market regimes (calm, GFC, European debt crisis, COVID-19). Uses 126-day rolling windows on S&P 500, DAX, and FTSE 100 daily returns. Shows that both the median and dispersion of correlations increase significantly during crisis periods, providing direct evidence against the CCC assumption.

Keywords: correlation distribution, market regimes, crisis, boxplot, CCC, correlation clustering, financial contagion

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: yfinance API (^GSPC, ^GDAXI, ^FTSE), 2004-2024

Output: ch5b_crisis_boxplot.pdf

Example: Median pairwise correlation rises from 0.45 in calm regimes to 0.78 during GFC. Dispersion also increases, reflecting heightened uncertainty in cross-market relationships.
