Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_news_impact_corr

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Plots the DCC news impact surface for the correlation between S&P 500 and DAX. The surface shows how pairwise standardized residuals (z_1, z_2) affect the next-period conditional correlation through the DCC Q_t equation. Demonstrates that joint shocks of the same sign increase correlation (contagion effect) while opposite-sign shocks decrease it (decoupling effect).

Keywords: DCC, news impact, correlation surface, contagion, decoupling, standardized residuals, shock transmission

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: DCC-GARCH estimated parameters from TSA_ch5b_dcc_estimation

Output: ch5b_news_impact_correlation.pdf

Example: Joint negative shocks of -2 standard deviations increase the S&P 500-DAX correlation by approximately 0.06, while opposite-sign shocks decrease it by a similar magnitude.
