Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_dcc_portfolio

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Constructs minimum-variance portfolios using DCC-GARCH, CCC-GARCH, and static (equal-weight) covariance estimates for S&P 500, DAX, and Gold. Compares cumulative performance, annualized volatility, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, and turnover across the three approaches. Demonstrates that DCC dynamic rebalancing reduces drawdown and improves risk-adjusted returns, especially during financial crises.

Keywords: DCC, CCC, GARCH, portfolio optimization, minimum variance, dynamic weights, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, diversification

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: yfinance API (^GSPC, ^GDAXI, GC=F), 2004-2024

Output: ch5b_dynamic_weights.pdf, ch5b_portfolio_comparison.pdf

Example: DCC portfolio increases gold allocation during GFC (2008) and COVID (2020) crises, achieving lower drawdown and higher Sharpe ratio than CCC or static portfolios.
