Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_spillover_heatmap

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Estimates bivariate BEKK(1,1) GARCH models for pairs of global equity indices (S&P 500, DAX, FTSE 100, Nikkei 225) and computes a volatility spillover heatmap. Spillover intensity is measured as |a_12|^2 + |b_12|^2 from the BEKK off-diagonal parameters. Also computes the Diebold-Yilmaz (2012) spillover table from a VAR on realized volatilities.

Keywords: multivariate GARCH, BEKK, volatility spillover, Diebold-Yilmaz, contagion, systemic risk, heatmap

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: yfinance API (^GSPC, ^GDAXI, ^FTSE, ^N225), 2004-2024

Output: ch5b_spillover_heatmap.pdf

Example: Volatility spillover heatmap showing S&P 500 as the dominant volatility exporter, strong DAX-FTSE interconnection, and Nikkei as most isolated market.
