Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_cov_decomp

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Decomposes the DCC conditional covariance H_t = D_t R_t D_t into its three components for S&P 500 and DAX: individual conditional volatilities (D_t diagonal elements), dynamic conditional correlation (R_t off-diagonal), and the resulting conditional covariance. Visualizes all three time series to illustrate how covariance changes are driven by both volatility spikes and correlation shifts.

Keywords: DCC, covariance decomposition, conditional volatility, conditional correlation, D_t R_t D_t, S&P 500, DAX

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: DCC-GARCH estimated from TSA_ch5b_dcc_estimation

Output: ch5b_covariance_decomp.pdf

Example: During GFC (2008), both volatilities and correlation spike simultaneously, causing the covariance to increase by a factor of 5x compared to pre-crisis levels.
