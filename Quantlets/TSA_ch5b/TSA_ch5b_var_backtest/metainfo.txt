Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_var_backtest

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Computes portfolio VaR (99%) for a 60/40 S&P 500/DAX portfolio using DCC-GARCH, CCC-GARCH, and static covariance. Backtests VaR using the Christoffersen (1998) unconditional coverage and independence tests. Analyzes VaR and violation rates across VIX-based market regimes (calm, turbulent, crisis). Demonstrates that DCC captures time-varying correlations and produces fewer clustered violations than CCC or static approaches.

Keywords: Value at Risk, VaR, backtesting, Christoffersen test, DCC, CCC, portfolio risk, VIX, market regimes, Expected Shortfall

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: yfinance API (^GSPC, ^GDAXI, ^VIX), 2004-2024

Output: ch5b_var_backtest.pdf, ch5b_var_regime.pdf

Example: CCC underestimates crisis VaR by up to 30% because it assumes constant correlations. DCC VaR rises automatically when VIX > 30, producing violation rates closer to the nominal 1%.
