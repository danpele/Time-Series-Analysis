Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_param_comparison

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Visualizes the curse of dimensionality in multivariate GARCH models by plotting the number of parameters as a function of the number of assets N for VEC, Diagonal VEC, BEKK, Diagonal BEKK, Scalar BEKK, CCC, and DCC. Demonstrates that only CCC and DCC remain feasible for portfolios with N > 10 assets, while full VEC and BEKK parameter counts grow as O(N^4) and O(N^2) respectively.

Keywords: curse of dimensionality, parameter count, multivariate GARCH, VEC, BEKK, CCC, DCC, scalability

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: none (computed analytically)

Output: ch5b_param_comparison.pdf

Example: For N=10: full VEC requires 1,485 parameters, BEKK 255, Diagonal BEKK 75, CCC 75, DCC 77. CCC/DCC are the only feasible options for N > 10.
