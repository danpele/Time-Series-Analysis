Name of Quantlet: TSA_ch5b_hedge_ratio

Published in: Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

Description: Computes dynamic minimum-variance hedge ratios for S&P 500 spot hedged with E-mini futures using DCC-GARCH, CCC-GARCH, and static OLS. Evaluates rolling hedging effectiveness (HE) over a 252-day window and compares the three approaches. Shows that DCC hedge ratios are more responsive to market regime changes and produce superior hedging effectiveness, especially during periods of financial stress.

Keywords: hedge ratio, minimum variance, DCC, CCC, hedging effectiveness, futures, E-mini, dynamic hedging, over-hedging

Author: Daniel Traian Pele

Datafile: yfinance API (^GSPC, ES=F), 2004-2024

Output: ch5b_hedge_ratio.pdf, ch5b_hedge_effectiveness.pdf

Example: DCC hedge ratio varies in [0.82, 1.08] reflecting real market regimes, while CCC produces a smoother but suboptimal ratio. DCC maintains HE > 95% even during crises.
